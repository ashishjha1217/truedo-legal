The full picture

Everything TrueDo does.

One private, beautiful app that replaces a dozen. Here's the whole toolkit.

Money & net worth

Accounts, transactions, budgets, goals and a live net-worth picture. No bank linking required.

Tasks & habits

Categories, reminders, three view modes, habits with streaks and a web Kanban board.

Health

AI food scanning with grams & macros, weight & steps, a health score, medals and Apple Health.

Private vault

Face ID + PIN, one board for notes, voice memos, sketches, documents and photos — encrypted and folder-organised.

Collaboration

iPhone + Web
