Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TrueDo ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and protects your information. By using TrueDo you agree to the practices described here.

Effective July 2, 2026

1. Who we are

TrueDo is a personal organiser that keeps your money, tasks, notes and private documents in one place. You can reach us any time at ashishjhadeveloper@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account email — when you sign in with email and password, or with Apple or Google, we collect the email address on that account so we can authenticate you and sync your data across devices. With Apple/Google sign-in we receive only your name and email as shared by Apple/Google — never your Apple ID or Google password.
  • Content you create — finances (accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, goals), tasks, notes, bookmarks, documents and photos you add, and any passwords or secrets you choose to save in the Vault.
  • On-device media you select — photos and files are only accessed when you explicitly pick them to add to the app.
  • Health information you log — if you use the Health tab: your health profile (age, sex, height, weight, goal weight, activity level, lifestyle habits), the food, weight and steps entries you log, and your groceries and favourite foods. You can erase all of it at any time from Health → Settings → Reset health data.
  • Apple Health (optional, read-only) — with your permission, the app reads your steps, heart rate and active energy from Apple Health to show them on your Health page and in your health score. We never write to Apple Health without asking, never use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing, and never share or sell it to third parties. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

We do NOT connect to your bank, we do NOT import transactions automatically, and we do NOT collect advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or browsing history.

3. Device permissions we ask for

Every permission is optional, requested only when you first use the feature that needs it, and the app keeps working if you decline. You can change any of them later in your device Settings.

  • Camera — only when you scan a meal with the AI food scanner or capture a photo or document for your Vault.
  • Photo library — only when you pick pictures to add to your gallery, notes or Vault.
  • Microphone — only while you talk to the AI assistant by voice or use voice typing.
  • Speech recognition — to turn what you say into text while you dictate.
  • Apple Health — read-only access to steps, heart rate and active energy, as described above.
  • Face ID — to unlock your private Vault. Biometrics are handled entirely by your device’s operating system; your face data never reaches the app or our servers.
  • Notifications — to deliver the reminders you set up (tasks, dues, daily brief). Fully configurable per type, or can be left off.

4. How your data is stored

When you are signed out, your data stays on your device. When you sign in, your data is synced to our backend (Google Firebase) and tied to your account so you can access it from more than one device.

  • Vault PINs are never stored in plain text — only a one-way salted hash is kept. Locked notes are encrypted with a key derived from your PIN, so their contents cannot be read from your synced data without it.
  • Data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard TLS.

5. How we use your information

  • To provide the app’s core features and sync your content across your devices.
  • To authenticate you when you sign in.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

6. Third-party services

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and Cloud Storage) to sign you in, host your synced data, and store the photos and documents you add. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, that respective provider authenticates you. Your data is processed by these providers only to deliver the app’s functionality, under their respective privacy terms.

AI assistant — TrueDo includes an optional AI assistant ("Ti") powered by Google’s Gemini API. When you choose to use it, only the content of your request is sent to Google to generate a reply: your typed message in chat, or your spoken audio and its transcript in voice mode, along with the relevant items from your own TrueDo data needed to answer.

  • Your identity is never shared — your name, email address and location are not sent to Google with your AI requests.
  • PIN / Face-ID-locked Vault items are never sent to the AI.
  • Food photos you scan are sent to the AI only to estimate the meal and its nutrition, and health details you have logged may be included when needed to answer a health question you ask. Apple Health data is never sent for advertising or any purpose other than answering your request.
  • Your requests are processed only to answer you and are NOT used to train Google’s models. Google acts as our service provider under the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • AI features are optional — you can use the app fully without them.

7. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your synced content (finances, tasks, notes, documents, health data and vault items) and signs you out. You can also erase just your health data from Health → Settings → Reset health data, or email ashishjhadeveloper@gmail.com to request deletion.

8. Children’s privacy

TrueDo is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date shown above.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email ashishjhadeveloper@gmail.com.
