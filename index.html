A vault that's actually private.

One board for notes, voice memos, photos, documents and links — lock the sensitive ones behind Face ID and a PIN. Locked content is encrypted with a key derived from your PIN — it never leaves your control in the clear.

  • Voice notes, hand-drawn sketches & folders
  • Private photo gallery
